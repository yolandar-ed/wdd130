Hello! My name is Yolanda Rodriguez and I am from El Salvador, La Libertad. I have lived in the country for 34 years While our tropical weather keeps things interesting, I love the convenience of my hometown. Everything I need—from supermarkets and shopping malls to theaters and the beautiful local beaches—is just a short drive away. Outside of my professional interests, I find joy and relaxation in the creative and organized worlds of cooking, baking, and keeping a beautiful home. I am currently studying to become a Software Developer, and I am absolutely thrilled to be expanding my technical skills. This journey is both an exciting new chapter and a wonderful refresher. When I was 15 years old in high school, I had my first encounter with coding through HTML. While I am honest about the fact that I forgot most of it over the years, stepping back into this world has unlocked great memories and ignited a fresh curiosity. I am highly motivated to rebuild my foundational knowledge, master modern development practices, and transition into the tech industry. I look forward to connecting with fellow students, mentors, and professionals who share this passion for learning and growth.
