Peru Rafting

Company Purpose: To open the wild, remote, and sacred waterways of Peru to the world. Our mission is to provide unparalleled, safe, and exhilarating river rafting and trekking experiences across Peru. Our Vision is to be the leading adventure travel company in Peru, renowned for our commitment to safety, sustainability, and unforgettable experiences. We believe in the transformative power of adventure, the importance of preserving our natural world, and the value of creating lasting memories for our clients. "Conquer the Rapids. Connect with the Culture."

History

Mayuc-Cusco and Peru Rafting plus trekking is a company created by native Peruvians about 37 years ago. We are the pioneers in rafting and trekking trips in southern Peru.
