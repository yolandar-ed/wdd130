Urubamba River!

Duration: Half Day | Difficulty: Class II - III

Perfect for beginners and families! Wind through the breathtaking Sacred Valley with gentle waves interspersed with exciting splashes. Includes a traditional riverside snack and complete safety gear briefing.

Apurimac Canyon Challenge

Duration: 3 Days | Difficulty: Class III - V

A true wilderness expedition. Paddle deep within a 3,000-meter-deep canyon wall formation running intense, continuous technical rapids. Camp under the stars on isolated pristine sandy beaches each evening.

Cotahuasi Extreme Expedition

Duration: 5 Days | Difficulty: Class IV - V

Designed strictly for veteran thrill-seekers. Tackle the deepest canyon corridor on earth. This high-octane journey offers non-stop technical rapids, breathtaking technical drops, and unforgettable ancient ruins exploration along the banks.
